About SmallSpaceFit

SmallSpaceFit exists for one kind of reader: you live in an apartment, you want to train at home, and every piece of fitness content you find assumes you have a garage.

What we do

We publish research-driven buying guides for compact, quiet, rental-friendly fitness equipment. For every guide we compare current models on their listed specifications — footprint, motor type, weight capacity, adjustment mechanisms — and on recurring themes in user feedback, with special weight on the constraints that only matter in an apartment: noise through the floor, storage when not in use, and floors you don't own.

What we don't do

We don't accept payment for placement in our guides, and we don't rank products by commission rate. Where we haven't physically tested a product, we say our analysis is research-based rather than pretending otherwise — a surprising amount of this category can be evaluated honestly from specifications, mechanism design, and aggregated owner experience.

How we make money

Through affiliate links. If you buy a product through a link on this site, the retailer may pay us a small commission at no extra cost to you. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. This funds the site; it doesn't decide the rankings. Details in our affiliate disclosure.

Contact

Spotted an error, a spec that changed, or a product we should look at? Open an issue on our GitHub repository — corrections are genuinely welcome.
